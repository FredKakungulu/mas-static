Elections

How the next committee gets chosen

Annual process

Elections are held once a year and open to all paid-up members. Voting runs on the official MAS booth — sign in with your student number and society email, receive a one-time code, then submit a secret ballot.

Between cycles, announcements and nominations still go out through the society's official channels. You can also verify a receipt after you vote.
